🎛️ Welcome to Roland JD-08 Total Commander
A high-performance patch librarian, binary backup editor, and deep sound design workspace built specifically for the Roland Boutique JD-08 and classic Roland JD-800 architecture.
🗂️
Dual Independent Pools
Work simultaneously with two full sound banks: Left Pool and Right Pool. Each pool manages 256 Patches (Banks A–D) and 128 Scenes (Banks 1–16). Compare, organize, and merge files effortlessly.
💾
Hardware-Accurate SVD5
Directly loads and saves standard Roland Boutique backup files (.SVD / SVD5). Supports loading single patch SysEx files and generating Roland-compatible backups without data loss.
🔌
Hardware USB Integration (Beta)
Connect your physical Roland JD-08 via USB. Click patches to instantly listen on the hardware synth, route audio to your PC speakers, and play notes live from your keyboard.
📂 Dual Pool Librarian & Drag-and-Drop Power
Organize, reorganize, and manage entire sound collections with speed and precision.
⇄
Swap & Copy Drag Modes
Toggle the drag mode in the header: ⇄ Swap exchanges positions of two slots; ➔ Copy duplicates the source patch into the destination slot. Drag within a pane or across panes.
🌉
Central Bridge Operations
The central strip gives you one-click batch tools: Copy ➔ (Left to Right), ⬅ Copy (Right to Left), ⇄ Swap, standard Clipboard tools, and 🏷️ Tagging.
🧹
Bank Tools: Sort & Compact
Click 🛠️ TOOLS on any bank header to open the tools drawer: sort alphabetically, sort by category tag, or 🧹 Compact to eliminate empty slots.
🔗
Auto-Relink Scenes
When enabled in the top header, moving or sorting patches in Banks A–D automatically updates all Scene Part A and Part B patch pointers so performance scenes never break.
🎛️ Deep 4-Tone Synthesizer Engine
Inspect, sculpt, and fine-tune every parameter of the legendary Roland JD-800 / JD-08 sound engine.
🧬
4-Tone Architecture
Every JD-08 patch is built from up to 4 discrete Tones (T1–T4). Each tone has its own Wave Generator, TVF Filter, TVA Amplifier, Multi-stage Envelopes, and dedicated LFOs.
📟
Overview Dock Console
The bottom dock bar gives you immediate real-time feedback. Click T1–T4 to solo or mute tones, adjust level meters, edit patch names, and manage clipboard items. Press Shift+Tab to collapse or expand.
🔬
Deep Control Surface
Press Space, Enter, or Double-Click any slot to open the Deep Inspector: full visual faders for Cutoff, Resonance, Envelopes, EQ, Chorus, Delay, and Reverb.
🔌 USB Hardware Integration & Sound Preview (Beta)
Audition patches, stream digital audio to PC speakers, and play notes live over USB.
⚡
One-Click USB Connect
Connect your Roland Boutique JD-08 with a standard USB cable and click Connect in the dock bar. Safe Web MIDI strictly connects to the JD-08 Sound Port.
🎵
Real-Time Preset Audition
When connected, clicking any patch slot in Left or Right pool immediately transmits Bank Select and Program Change to the physical JD-08, allowing instant listening.
🔈
PC Speaker Audio Monitor
Click 🔈 PC SPK in the dock bar to route the JD-08's USB digital audio directly to your computer speakers or headphones using Web Audio API!
🎹
Live QWERTY Keyboard
Play notes live using your computer's keyboard (keys A, W, S, E, D, F, G, Y, H, U, J, K) to test and audition presets anytime with dynamic velocity.
